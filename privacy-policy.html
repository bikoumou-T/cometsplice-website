Comet Splice privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-12 · CometSplice

Summary

Comet Splice does not sell or share personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases or chat. The whole game works offline. The only thing that ever goes online is the optional ranking, and only after you join it.

Online rankings (only if you join)

Rankings are off until you choose Rankings → Join, after a grown-up's OK. When you join, the game sends our ranking server: a random player number made by the game with a private key, a nickname the game makes from two words and a number (you never type it), the country you pick, and your best scores for Endless, Levels, Daily and Weekly. Other players see your nickname, country flag and scores. Nothing else is sent: no name, e-mail, contacts, location or device details. The server is run for us by Supabase and the data is sent encrypted. Old Daily and Weekly scores are removed after 60 days. If you add friends with friend codes, the server also keeps a short friend code for you and the list of players you linked with. Friends see the same nickname, flag and scores as everyone else. Removing a friend or leaving the rankings deletes the link. Choose Rankings → Leave rankings at any time and your nickname and every score are deleted from the server.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress and settings (scores, stars, medals, restoration progress, cosmetics, language and display options) in the app's own storage on your device. It also creates a random support number (the “Customer ID”) on your device so you can quote it if you report a problem. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Things that only happen when you choose

Result card. After a Daily or Weekly run, a grown-up can open “Share result card” (behind a simple adult check) and copy it or send it with your phone's share menu. The card contains only the mode, the date and an icon strip of catches, hits and relays: no score, name or course data. It goes only to the app you pick, under that app's own privacy terms.

Course code. The same adult check guards the optional course code, which contains the course, your score and your sampled route so a friend can race it. You copy it yourself.

Backup code. Settings → Back up progress makes a text code of your progress and settings for you to keep. It is made on your device, never sent to us, and doesn't include your rankings key.

Report a problem. This builds a text you can copy. It includes the game version, your Customer ID, the date, the screen you were on, your device's browser description and the last few wrong contacts in the game. Nothing is sent automatically; you decide whether to send it to support@cometsplice.com.

Children

Comet Splice is made for everyone, including children. It does not ask anyone for personal information. Joining the rankings needs a grown-up's OK first, uses only a made-up nickname and a country, and can be undone at any time. The adult check is a simple speed bump, not a verification of parental consent.

Device backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the game's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for other apps. This is controlled by your device settings, and we cannot see it.

Permissions

The Android app asks for vibration (optional haptic feedback, which you can switch off in Settings) and internet access, which is used only for the rankings after you join. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or advertising ID.

Deleting your data

Choose Rankings → Leave rankings to delete your nickname and scores from the server straight away. Use Settings → Reset local progress to erase your progress on this device, or clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. If you can no longer open the game, e-mail us your nickname and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and at the policy's web address, with a new effective date.
